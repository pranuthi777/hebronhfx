Who We Are

About Zion
Telugu Church

We are a people who love Jesus Christ — a Spirit-knit family of believers in the heart of Halifax, Nova Scotia. However you arrive, you arrive home.

ZION TELUGU CHURCH is an independent church in Halifax, Nova Scotia. We warmly welcome individuals and families from all walks of life who desire to know, love, and follow the Lord Jesus Christ.

Our fellowship is grounded in Acts 2:42 — devoted to the apostles' teaching, fellowship, the breaking of bread, and prayer. Drawing inspiration from the biblical principles exemplified in the ministry of Bro. Bakht Singh, we are committed to faithfully proclaiming the Gospel, living according to God's Word, and nurturing a Christ-centered community of believers.

We preach Jesus Christ — crucified, risen, and coming again — declaring that salvation is found through Him alone. We hold firmly to the authority of the Holy Scriptures, the transforming power of the Holy Spirit, and God's call for His people to live holy lives and serve as faithful witnesses in these last days.

Our mission is to strengthen the Body of Christ through sound, Bible-based teaching, heartfelt worship, earnest prayer, genuine fellowship, and intentional discipleship. We seek to equip every believer to serve the Lord faithfully and to carry the hope of the Gospel both locally and to the ends of the earth.

Whether you are exploring the Christian faith, newly arrived in Halifax, or simply seeking a Telugu church family — you are warmly invited to worship, grow, and serve alongside us as we glorify God and make the Lord Jesus Christ known.

What We Hold

Our Core Beliefs

  1. 01

    Salvation by Grace

    Salvation is a free gift, received by grace through faith in Jesus Christ alone.

  2. 02

    The Holy Trinity

    We believe in one God, eternally existing as the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit.

  3. 03

    The Supreme Authority of Scripture

    The Holy Scriptures are the supreme authority for faith and life — God-breathed, true, and sufficient.

  4. 04

    The Power of the Holy Spirit

    We believe in the power and gifts of the Holy Spirit, given to build up the church and witness to the world.

  5. 05

    End-Time Revival & Missions

    We look expectantly for end-time revival and the advance of global missions as we await our Lord's return.

  6. 06

    The Unity of the Body

    We cherish the unity of the Body of Christ — one church, living and serving together under one Head.

How It All Began

A Church Born in Prayer

  1. 45 days

    Fasting · Prayer · Waiting

    After 45 days of fasting, prayer, and waiting upon the Lord, God confirmed His call to establish His church in Halifax.

  2. The call

    1 Chronicles 28:10

    “Consider now, for the LORD has chosen you to build a house for the sanctuary; be strong, and do it.”

  3. The confirmation

    1 Chronicles 28:20 · Haggai 2:4–5

    “Be strong and of good courage, and do it; do not fear nor be dismayed, for the LORD God—my God—will be with you.” “…be strong, all you people of the land,' says the LORD, 'and work; for I am with you.”

  4. Where we are

    Zion Telugu Church, Halifax

    With this clear confirmation, we began gathering together at the feet of the Lord Jesus — uniting around His Word, His presence, and His purpose for this city.

Our Foundation

Acts 2:42 — A Blueprint for Fellowship

“And they continued steadfastly in the apostles' doctrine and fellowship, in the breaking of bread, and in prayers.”

— Acts 2:42

At Zion Telugu Church, this foundational scripture is more than a verse — it is our way of life. We are a community devoted to growing in faith, deepening relationships with one another, and serving God together in Halifax and beyond.

01

Apostles' Teaching

“Hold fast the pattern of sound words which you have heard from me, in faith and love which are in Christ Jesus.”
— 2 Timothy 1:13

Rooted firmly in Scripture, our teaching is clear, biblically grounded, and transformative — equipping believers to live according to God's Word.

02

Fellowship

“Not forsaking the assembling of ourselves together…”
— Hebrews 10:25

We are more than a congregation — we are a Spirit-knit family of believers, bound together in love, encouragement, and a shared devotion to Christ.

03

Breaking of Bread

“Let a man examine himself, and so let him eat of the bread and drink of the cup.”
— 1 Corinthians 11:28

Every Sunday, we gather to reflect on God's Word and partake in Communion together — proclaiming Christ's sacrifice and our living hope in His resurrection.

04

Prayers

“Continue earnestly in prayer, being vigilant in it with thanksgiving.”
— Colossians 4:2

United in prayer, we faithfully intercede for one another, for our community, for our nation, and for the world — trusting in God's power to move.

Reaching Out
& Raising Up

At Zion Telugu Church, we are called to engage our community and invest in the next generation of believers. Our ministries extend beyond the church walls — bringing the message of Christ to the streets of Halifax and building a lasting foundation of faith in our youth.

Street Preaching

Our Street Preaching team takes the Gospel directly to the heart of Halifax, proclaiming the good news of Jesus Christ where people live and gather. We are committed to being a light in the city — offering hope, extending grace, and inviting all to encounter the transforming love of God.

Ministry · Elders

Seniors Ministry

Our Seniors Ministry honours and cherishes the wisdom, faith, and experience of our elder members. We create a warm, nurturing space where seniors can enjoy meaningful fellowship, receive pastoral care, and continue to grow in their walk with God. Through regular gatherings, prayer support, and community visits, we ensure that every senior feels valued, loved, and deeply connected to the family of Zion Telugu Church.
